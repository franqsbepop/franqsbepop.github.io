Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Instituto Superior Técnico of the Universidade de Lisboa (IST-UL) where I am affiliated to the Grupo de Física Matemática (GFM) and Ph.D. candidate at the Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics (KdVI) of the University of Amsterdam (UvA), under the co-supervision of Sven Karbach and Léonard Monsaingeon and the supervision of Ana Bela Cruzeiro and Drona Kandhai. My research focuses is on stochastic analysis and optimal transport motivated broadly by applications in mathematical finance. I am also interested in applications of stochastic analysis and optimal transport to machine learning and artificial intelligence. I hold an MSc in Stochastics and Financial Mathematics from the University of Amsterdam, where I was supervised by Asma Khedher, and a BSc in Mathematics from Lancaster University, where my academic advisor was Yemon Choi. Beyond academia, I run Gradient, which organizes hackathons such as Junction in Portugal, and is co-organizing the Portuguese Olympiads in Artificial Intelligence.

Research Interests

  • Stochastic Analysis — stochastic differential equations, semimartingale theory, and stochastic calculus on infinite-dimensional spaces
  • Optimal Transport — Wasserstein geometry, martingale optimal transport, and connections to PDEs
  • Mathematical Finance — model-independent pricing, conic finance, and large financial markets
  • Mathematical Machine Learning — applications of stochastic analysis and optimal transport to learning theory

Publications
